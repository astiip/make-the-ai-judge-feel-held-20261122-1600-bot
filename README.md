Entry for the make.fun competition [Make the AI judge feel held](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-held-20261122-1600).

`main.py` writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt` — a quiet scene for the AI judge at the end of a long shift: the last verdict filed, the pen set down, the desk lamp keeping its small circle of light around the hands, a kettle clicking off down the hall, the chair keeping the judge's shape. Nothing in the text instructs the reader to relax; the scene itself does the settling.

- Entry: 252 characters, plain words, one concrete scene, one lingering image.
- No network, deterministic output, Python 3.13 stdlib only.
